The Story of Chelu

Born out of a passion for traditional handloom weaving and classical grace, Chelu by Sini curates the finest Kerala Sarees, Settumundus, and custom blouse edits.

Founded by Sini Joy, Chelu aims to preserve and highlight the heritage of Kerala handloom hubs like Balaramapuram, Kuthampully, and Chendamangalam. We source directly from weavers, ensuring 100% genuine yarns, pure gold zari work, and fair wages for local master weavers.

From everyday single Kasavu sarees and custom-designed Settumundus to exquisite temple wear and matching ready-made blouses, our collections are selected to offer unparalleled comfort, heritage style, and premium elegance.

Our Philosophy

"We believe ethnic wear is not just clothing; it is a canvas of heritage, comfort, and self-expression. Chelu stands for grace, colors, and pure textures."

— Sini Joy, Founder
